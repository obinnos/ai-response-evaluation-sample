## Sample Task

**Prompt given to AI:** "Explain why the sky is blue in simple terms for a 10-year-old."

**AI Response (to evaluate):** "The sky is blue because the ocean reflects its colour up to the sky."

## My Evaluation

### 1. Instruction Following: 2 / 5
It tried to answer simply, but it failed the main task — to explain correctly for a 10-year-old.

### 2. Factual Accuracy: 1 / 5
This is a common myth. The sky is not blue because of the ocean. The real reason is that air scatters blue light from the sun more than other colours.

### 3. Logic / Reasoning: 2 / 5
If the ocean made the sky blue, then deserts and land areas would have a different sky colour. They don't. The logic breaks.

### 4. Clarity & Tone: 3 / 5
The sentence is short and easy to read, which is good for a child. But it is confidently wrong, which is worse than being confusing.

## Verdict: 2 / 5

**Why:** Simple to read, but factually wrong and logically weak. A child would walk away with the wrong idea.

**How to Fix It:** "The sky looks blue because when sunlight hits the air, the tiny bits in the air scatter the blue part of the light all around — like blue paint spreading in water."
